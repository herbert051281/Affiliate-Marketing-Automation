# 01 — Strategy & Business Model

## The decision that matters most

Affiliate marketing is not one business. It's five, and they have wildly different
automation profiles. Picking wrong costs you six months.

| Model | Time to $1 | Automation fit | Durability | Capital needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Recurring-commission SaaS/tools in a vertical | 3–6 weeks | High | **Very high** | ~$150/mo |
| B. Programmatic comparison site | 4–9 months | **Very high** | High | ~$200/mo |
| C. Buyer-intent newsletter | 4–8 weeks | High | **Very high** (owned) | ~$150/mo |
| D. Short-form video → link in bio | 1–3 weeks | Medium-high | Low (platform risk) | ~$100/mo |
| E. Paid ads → offer arbitrage | Days | High | Low | $3k+ at risk |

### Recommendation: run A + C as the core, D as the traffic engine, B as the moat.

**Skip E** unless you want to risk real capital. Paid arbitrage is the fastest path to
revenue *and* the fastest path to a $3,000 hole. Revisit it in month 4, funded by
profit, once you know your conversion rate — at that point it's math, not gambling.

**Do not build on Amazon Associates.** 24-hour cookie, 1–4% commissions, no email links
allowed, and rates get cut unilaterally. Fine as a secondary link, fatal as a foundation.

## Why recurring commission is the whole ballgame

One-time commission: sell 20/month, you must sell 20 again next month forever.

Recurring commission: sell 20/month at $15/mo recurring, and month 12 you're collecting
on ~200 accumulated subscriptions before you sell anything new.

```
One-time ($60/sale, 20 sales/mo):     Month 1: $1,200   Month 12: $1,200
Recurring ($15/mo, 20 sales/mo, 8% churn):
                                      Month 1:   $300   Month 12: ~$2,400
```

Recurring wins by month 5 and never stops. It's also the model that best justifies an
automation build: the asset compounds while the machine runs.

**Target offer profile:**
- 20–40% **recurring** commission (or 30%+ one-time above $100)
- Cookie window ≥ 60 days
- Merchant is a real SaaS with low churn and a self-serve trial
- Public affiliate program with an API or at least a CSV export (needed for automated
  conversion ingestion — see [Measurement](06-measurement.md))
- TOS permits: content sites, email, and organic social. Check each explicitly.

## Why a newsletter, not just a website

Google traffic is rented. AI answer engines increasingly resolve queries without a click.
An email list is the only asset in this business you own outright.

It also fixes the monetization math: a website visitor monetizes once. A subscriber
monetizes every week for years, across *multiple* offers, at a click-through rate 10–40×
higher than cold search traffic.

The whole funnel:

```
Short video / search → landing page → free lead magnet → email list
                                                           │
                                          weekly automated issue
                                                           │
                                     ┌─────────────────────┴───────────────┐
                                     │  soft offer   deep review   tool of  │
                                     │   in issue      article      the week│
                                     └──────────────────┬──────────────────┘
                                                  tracked link
                                                        │
                                                  merchant → commission
```

## Why short-form video is the traffic engine

- Cost per view is near zero; algorithmic distribution means zero domain authority needed.
- It's the only channel where a brand-new account can reach 100k people in week one.
- It's now fully automatable end-to-end: script → voice → footage → captions → schedule.
- **Platform risk is real** — which is exactly why its job is to *feed the email list*,
  not to be the business. Every video drives to the lead magnet.

Volume plan: 3–5 shorts/day across TikTok + Reels + YouTube Shorts, all repurposed from
content you're already producing. Zero incremental research cost.

## Why programmatic comparison pages are the moat

The highest-commercial-intent queries are structural, not creative:

- `best [tool] for [use case]`
- `[tool A] vs [tool B]`
- `[tool] pricing / alternatives / free plan limits`

These are **data problems, not writing problems** — which is why they automate beautifully
and why they're what AI assistants cite when a buyer asks for a recommendation. Build a
structured database of tools (features, pricing, limits, integrations) and generate pages
from it. Update the data, every page updates. That's leverage no content mill has.

**Critical:** these pages must contain something only you have — your own pricing tracker,
your own tested screenshots, your own scoring methodology. Generic AI-generated comparison
pages are exactly what search engines now suppress. See [Compliance & Risk](07-compliance-and-risk.md).

## What "automated" honestly means here

| Fully automated (no human) | You approve (seconds) | Stays human (rare) |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword/trend/competitor intake | Topic batch (10 at a time) | Niche choice (once) |
| Offer discovery + EPC monitoring | Drafts flagged by QA | Merchant relationships |
| Brief generation | Spend changes | Brand voice reset (quarterly) |
| Draft writing + fact-check pass | Kill/scale calls (monthly) | Anything legal |
| Image/video/caption generation | New offer additions | |
| Publishing, scheduling, repurposing | | |
| Link health, offer swapping | | |
| Click/conversion ingestion, reporting | | |

Target: **~15 minutes/day**, plus 30 minutes on Monday for the weekly scorecard.

## The uncomfortable truths

1. **Automation does not create demand.** If the niche has no buyers, a perfect machine
   produces perfect zero. Selection (doc 02) matters more than the build.
2. **Scaled AI content without differentiation gets suppressed.** The approval gates and
   the original-data requirement aren't bureaucracy — they're the reason this survives.
3. **Cash flow lags 30–60 days.** Networks pay net-30 to net-60 after month close. A sale
   in week 3 might be cash in week 12. Budget runway accordingly.
4. **You will want to widen the niche in month 2. Don't.** One niche, one brand, until
   it's profitable. Then clone the whole machine for niche #2 — the system is the asset.
