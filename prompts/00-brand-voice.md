## Voice

| Dimension | Setting |
|---|---|
| Person | First person plural ("we tested"), second person to reader ("you") |
| Reading level | Grade 8–9. Short sentences. |
| Stance | Opinionated. We give verdicts, not "it depends". |
| Humor | Dry, sparing. Never at the reader's expense. |
| Hedging | Minimal. "Probably" only when genuinely uncertain. |

## Rules

1. Lead with the verdict. The recommendation goes in the first 60 words.
2. Say who a tool is **wrong** for. Every review names at least one disqualifier.
3. Date every price and every feature claim.
4. Never claim to have tested something we haven't.
5. Prefer a specific number to an adjective. "Caps at 2,000 rows" beats "somewhat limited".

## Banned phrases

`in today's fast-paced world` · `game-changer` · `unlock the power of` · `dive into` ·
`it's important to note` · `when it comes to` · `look no further` · `in conclusion` ·
`whether you're a X or a Y` · `the world of [niche]`

## Example — the standard to hit

**Bad:**
> When it comes to choosing project management software, there are many options
> available. In this comprehensive guide, we'll dive into the top choices.

**Good:**
> If you're a two-person agency, use [Tool A]. It's $12/seat, it does the three things
> you actually need, and you'll be set up in an afternoon. Skip it if you bill hourly —
> its time tracking caps at 5 active timers, which we hit in week two.
